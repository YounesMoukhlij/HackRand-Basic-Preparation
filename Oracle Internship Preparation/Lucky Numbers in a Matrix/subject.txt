
### **Lucky Numbers in a Matrix**

You are given an `m x n` matrix of distinct integers.

A **lucky number** is an element of the matrix that is **the minimum in its row** and at the same time **the maximum in its column**.

Your task is to find all lucky numbers in the matrix.

---

### **Input Format**

* The first line contains two integers `m` and `n` — the number of rows and columns.
* The next `m` lines each contain `n` integers, representing the matrix.

---

### **Constraints**

* `1 <= m, n <= 50`
* All elements are distinct and fit in a 32-bit integer.

---

### **Output Format**

* Print all the lucky numbers separated by a space.
* If there are no lucky numbers, print nothing (or an empty line).

---

### **Example 1**

**Input**

```
3 3
3 7 8
9 11 13
15 16 17
```

**Output**

```
15
```

**Explanation**

* Row 1 min = 3, col max = no.
* Row 2 min = 9, col max = no.
* Row 3 min = 15, and it’s also the maximum of its column → ✅ lucky number.

---

### **Example 2**

**Input**

```
2 2
1 10
5 7
```

**Output**

```
7
```
